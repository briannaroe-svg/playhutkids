Dashboard Help

What each tab does, and who can see it.

Two access levels: Admin can see and do everything below. Everyone means both admin and regular staff accounts can access that tab, though what they see inside it often differs — noted in each section.

Overview

The sidebar, top to bottom: Home, Families, Children, Registration, Documents, Services, Billing, Scheduling, Timesheets, and Staff Accounts. Below the tab list is a standing clock-in card, always visible, separate from the tabs above it.

A staff account only sees tabs relevant to them — Home, Children, Documents, Scheduling, and Timesheets — plus the clock-in card. Admin accounts see every tab.

Home Everyone

Lands here right after logging in.

A quick daily briefing: today's date, today's posted events, who's scheduled to work today (admins see everyone, staff see their own shift), and a preview of everything scheduled — events and shifts — over the next 7 days. It's read-only; making changes still happens on Scheduling.

Families Admin

The parent/guardian contact address book.

Every family record holds a primary parent's name, email, phone, and mailing address, plus an optional secondary parent. This is where billing contact info lives — invoices are always addressed to a family, not directly to a child.

What you can do here

  • Search existing families by parent name or email
  • + Add family to create a new record by hand
  • Click Edit on any row to update contact details
Note: Families don't need to be created here one-by-one — the Registration tab creates a family and a child together in one signed step. Use Families directly when you just need to update contact info, or add a family without going through a full registration.

Children Everyone

Enrollment roster, room assignment, and daily check-in/check-out.

Every enrolled child belongs to a room. Everyone — admin and staff — currently sees the full roster, since staff aren't yet tied to a specific room themselves. As staffing grows and staff get assigned to a room, this view is expected to start filtering by that.

The rooms

  • Little Bunnies (Infant & 1 year olds)
  • Little Raccoons (2 year olds)
  • Little Cubs (3 & 4 year olds)
  • 3 Year Old Preschool-AM
  • 3 Year Old Preschool-PM
  • 4 Year Old Preschool-AM
  • 4 Year Old Preschool-PM
  • Wolf Den (School Age Kids)

For admins

  • See every actively enrolled child, their room, and today's check-in status
  • + Add child for a quick roster entry (for a formal signed enrollment, use Registration instead)
  • Edit a child to update their info or change their room
  • Check any child in or out
  • Daily Report — fill out today's report for that child (see below)

For staff

  • See the full roster, grouped by room, with today's status
  • Click a child's name to view their allergies, medical notes, and emergency contact — read-only, and parent contact info is intentionally left out of this view
  • Check a child in when they arrive, check them out when they leave
  • Daily Report — fill out today's report for that child (see below)
Allergy safety check: If a child has an allergy on file, checking them in shows a red confirmation popup with the exact allergy text. The check-in doesn't complete until I understand is clicked. Children with no allergies on file check in instantly with no popup.

Daily Reports

Everyone — admin and staff — can fill out or update a child's report for today: a meal checklist (Breakfast, Lunch, PM Snack, each with optional notes), nap start and end time, a tap-to-select mood, free-text notes, and a photo. Filling it out again later in the day updates the same report rather than creating a second one — add a nap time after lunch was already logged, for instance, without losing what was entered earlier.

These are what parents see in the Family Portal, so this is the one place in the dashboard that's genuinely parent-facing content, even though it's filled out from the staff side.

Registration Admin

Formal, signed enrollment — creates the family and child records together.

This is the intended way to enroll a new family: one form captures both the parent/guardian info and the child's info, and nothing is created in the system until a signature is collected. A registration sits as pending until it's signed, at which point the family and child records are created automatically and a signed PDF is generated.

How to use it

  1. Click + Add new Registration and fill out the family and child sections
  2. Save it — it now shows as pending in the list, nothing enrolled yet
  3. Choose how it gets signed: Sign now opens a signature pad for the parent to sign in person on your screen, or Send link generates a remote signing link
  4. Once signed, the family and child are created automatically, and a signed PDF is attached to the record

A new child created this way doesn't have a room set yet — assign one afterward from the Children tab.

Remote signing: "Send link" generates a 72-hour link a parent can open on their own phone or computer. It shows them the same family and child details entered here, lets them sign on their own screen, and completes the enrollment the moment they submit — no need for them to be physically present.

Documents Everyone

Employee handbook agreements, and — for admins — every signed document in the system.

This is distinct from the daycare/preschool handbooks that parents sign during registration — this covers the employee handbook, which every staff member (including admins) signs for themselves.

For everyone

  • See your own handbook documents — pending ones show a Sign button that opens a signature pad; signed ones link to the signed PDF

For admins, additionally

  • + Add handbook version — posting a new version automatically creates a pending agreement for every active staff member, so nobody needs to be manually assigned
  • Who's signed? next to each handbook version shows a checklist of who has and hasn't signed it yet
  • An All signed documents table showing every signed document across the whole system — both staff handbook signatures and parent daycare/preschool agreements — each tagged Staff or Parent, with a link to the PDF

Services Admin

A reusable catalog of billable line items.

Rather than typing "Monthly Tuition" or "Late Pickup Fee" from scratch on every invoice, add them here once with a default price. When building an invoice, picking a service auto-fills its name and price onto the line — still editable per invoice if the amount needs to change.

What you can do here

  • + Add service — name, optional description, default price
  • Edit a service, including marking it inactive so it stops showing up as an option on new invoices (past invoices that already used it are unaffected)

Billing Admin

Invoices, payment links, and payment status.

Creating an invoice

  1. Click + New invoice
  2. Search for the family it's billing
  3. Add line items — pick from the Services catalog (auto-fills price) or choose Custom… to type a one-off item
  4. Save — the invoice starts in draft status

Getting paid

  • Get payment link creates a real Stripe payment link and copies it to your clipboard — this uses live Stripe, so it charges real money
  • Copy link re-copies a link that already exists for that invoice
  • Mark paid manually flips an invoice to paid — useful for cash or check payments, or any payment collected outside Stripe
Note: There's no automatic sync between a Stripe payment and this dashboard yet — even if a family pays through the link, an admin still needs to click Mark paid to reflect that here. Automatic syncing is built on the backend but not turned on.

Scheduling Everyone

Shifts, school-wide events, and time-off requests.

For admins

  • + Add shift — assign a staff member to a specific date and time
  • + Add event — post something everyone should see, like a staff meeting or a closure day
  • Review time-off requests below the calendar — Approve or Deny each one. A small badge on the Scheduling tab itself shows how many requests are waiting
  • Click Who's seen this? on any event to see which staff have acknowledged it

For staff

  • See your own upcoming shifts, plus every event admin has posted
  • Request time off — submit dates and an optional reason; it shows up for admins to review, along with your own request history and its status
Event popups: When admin posts a new event, every staff member sees a one-time popup the next time they log in, describing the event. They must click I understand to dismiss it — this is what admin is checking in "Who's seen this?"

Timesheets Everyone

Clock-in history, break/lunch times, and payroll export. Actual clocking in and out happens from the clock-in card in the sidebar, not from this tab.

For admins

  • See a live list of who's currently clocked in, at the top of the page
  • See every staff member's timesheet entries, filterable by date range, including each break/lunch period they took that shift
  • Edit an entry to correct a wrong clock-in or clock-out time
  • Delete an entry entirely if it shouldn't exist — this is a permanent action
  • Download PDF — a payroll-ready report for the selected date range, grouped by staff member with totals, ready to send to an accountant

For staff

  • See only your own entries, filterable by date range, including your break/lunch times
  • View-only — no editing or deleting
Breaks are unpaid: Time spent on a break or lunch is automatically subtracted from the total hours shown here and in the payroll PDF — it doesn't count as worked time.

Staff Accounts Admin

Who can log in, and what they can see.

What you can do here

  • + Add staff account — name, email, role/title, hourly rate, an optional clock-in PIN, and a password they'll use to log in
  • Toggle Admin access when creating or editing an account — this is what decides whether they see the full sidebar or just Home, Children, Documents, Scheduling, and Timesheets
  • Edit to update details or reset a password
  • Delete removes their ability to log in, but keeps all their historical timesheet entries and any invoices or records tied to them fully intact — nothing is erased
Note: You can't delete your own account, and an already-deactivated account won't show a Delete button — there'd be nothing left to remove.

Clock In / Break Everyone

Not a tab — a standing card at the bottom of the sidebar, below the tab list.

A status line shows whether you're clocked in (and since when), or on a break. Two small buttons below it handle the actions: Clock in / Clock out, and Break / End break. The break button is disabled until you're clocked in, and Clock out is disabled while you're on a break — end the break first.

No PIN is needed for any of this — you're already identified by being logged in. Break and lunch time is automatically excluded from your paid hours; see Timesheets for where that shows up.

Family Portal Admin sets up access

A separate, parent-facing page — not part of the staff dashboard.

Parents log in at portal.html with their own email and password to see their child's daily reports — meals, nap times, mood, notes, and photos. It has its own login screen, its own session, and its own identity type entirely separate from staff accounts: a parent's login can never be used to access anything in the staff dashboard, and a staff login can never get into the portal.

Giving a family access

No family has portal access by default — an admin has to set a password for them first, from either place:

  • Families tab — editing an existing family shows a Portal password field. Leave it blank to change nothing; enter a password to set or reset their access. The hint text says whether they already have access set up.
  • Registration tab — once a registration is signed, its row gets a Set portal password action, so a new family can be given portal access right after enrolling.

Give the family their email (whatever's on file as the primary parent email) and the password you set — that's all they need to sign in.

What a parent sees

  • If they have more than one enrolled child, a row of name chips to switch between them
  • Every daily report for the selected child, most recent first — meals shown as eaten/skipped pills, nap time, a mood tag, any notes, and photos (tap one to view it larger)
View-only: Parents can't post anything, message staff, or edit their own info from the portal — it's strictly a window into what staff have already recorded.
